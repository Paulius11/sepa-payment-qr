SEPA Payment QR

Fill the transfer once, get a QR your bank app scans to auto-fill the payment. Works with Revolut, SEB, Swedbank, N26, Wise and any bank that reads the European EPC QR standard (EPC069-12 / “GiroCode”). Euro transfers only.

        About
        

          SEPA Payment QR turns bank-transfer details, an uploaded
          bill, or any link into a QR your phone or bank app can scan. Payments use
          the European EPC069-12
          standard (“SEPA Credit Transfer QR” / GiroCode) — the same one Revolut,
          SEB, Swedbank, N26 and Wise read. Fill it once, or drop a bill and let it
          prefill; style the code and add a logo; switch to “Link / text” for any
          URL. Everything runs in your browser — nothing you type leaves the page.
          Free and open source.
